# Deep-research prompt — one input at a time

Use for one external estimate about the PrimGrade niche, not for inventing
product performance. Replace the placeholders before sending.

> I am estimating **<one input>** for **<population, place, and year>** related
> to university physics teachers grading handwritten exam answers.
>
> 1. Give the best available figures with definition, population, year, method,
>    sample size, uncertainty, and the primary-source URL.
> 2. Show disagreements and explain differences in definition, method, or year.
> 3. Give a defensible low/high range and label assumptions.
> 4. Say what could not be found. Do not fill gaps with an estimate.

Return a table, the low/high range, and sources. Before entering a result in the
ledger as `research`, open at least two primary sources and verify the same
definition. Do not use this prompt to estimate PrimGrade accuracy, time saved,
conversion, revenue, or willingness to pay unless those are directly measured.
