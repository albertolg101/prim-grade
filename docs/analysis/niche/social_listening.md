# Social listening — research instruction

Input: an export of public, anonymized posts or comments about marking
university physics exams, handwritten answers, physics diagrams, grading
workload, OCR, or educational AI.

1. Remove duplicates, advertisements, links without substantive text, and
   personal identifiers. Report how many records remain.
2. Find 6–10 topics with three short exact quotes each and the share of
   records. Do not identify authors or students.
3. Count records describing each candidate niche in
   [`niche_brief.md`](./niche_brief.md), and separately count pain signals such
   as marking backlog, inconsistency, unreadable handwriting, diagram
   difficulty, or distrust of automated grading.
4. List jobs and workarounds in the teachers' own words, including manual
   marking, assistants, spreadsheets, LMS tools, and generic AI.
5. State which target users and institutions are absent. Public conversations
   are not a sample of buyers.

Return:

- a topic table with count, share, and evidence kind;
- a candidate-niche × mentions × pains table;
- five exact quotes that could change a product decision;
- limitations, privacy actions, and the next research question.

Never paraphrase a quote as exact. Never treat sentiment or mention counts as
evidence of willingness to pay or grading accuracy.
